1. Overview

GS1 Youtube Data ("the App") is an internal tool operated by Game Studio 1 – VNG Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App accesses through Google APIs and how that data is used.

2. Data we access

When an authorized user signs in with Google and grants permission, the App accesses the following through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, using read-only scopes:

  • Channel information: channel name, channel ID, and public channel statistics.
  • Channel analytics: aggregated metrics such as views, watch time, subscribers gained/lost, and engagement.

Scopes requested:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly

The App does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or any other Google account data.

3. How we use the data

  • Data is used solely to build internal reporting and social listening dashboards for YouTube channels owned by VNG Games.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share the data with any third party.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or to build user profiles.
  • We do not upload, modify, or delete any content on the connected channels.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens and retrieved data are stored only on VNG Games' internal systems with access restricted to authorized staff. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are applied to protect the data from unauthorized access.

5. Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as needed for internal reporting. You can revoke the App's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of stored data, contact us at the email below.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App also complies with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
