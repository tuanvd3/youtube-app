About

GS1 Youtube Data is an internal application used by Game Studio 1 (VNG Games) to read performance statistics of YouTube channels owned and operated by VNG Games. The data is used for internal social listening and reporting dashboards.

What the app does

  • Reads basic channel information (channel name, channel ID, public statistics) via the YouTube Data API v3.
  • Reads channel analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers, etc.) via the YouTube Analytics API.

Scopes requested

The app requests read-only access only:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly

The app never uploads, edits, deletes content or changes any channel settings.

Who can use it

Only authorized VNG Games staff who own or manage the company's official YouTube channels. The app is not offered to the public.
